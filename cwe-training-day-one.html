CWE Training, Day 1

The first day of the CWE preparation track focused on scanning, enumeration and finding weaknesses systematically. I worked on extractPorts scripting and network discovery alongside the course outline.

Modules covered

  • Scanning and enumeration
  • Identifying weaknesses
  • Path traversal
  • Privilege escalation vectors
  • Initial exploitation concepts

Practice focus

The emphasis was on building a repeatable assessment method: organize discovery results, investigate potential weaknesses, and connect findings to the next stage of a controlled test.

Consistent enumeration and clear notes make it easier to explain findings and reproduce a result in an authorized training or assessment environment.
